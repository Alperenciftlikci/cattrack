Common questions

Frequently asked

Is the AI as accurate as a vet?

No — Mochi is an early-warning tool, not a diagnostic device. The AI flags patterns and concerns to discuss with your vet, who remains the authority on medical decisions.

Does Mochi work with multiple cats?

Yes. Add unlimited cat profiles. The compatibility checker also helps multi-cat households read dynamics between cats.

Is my data private?

Yes. Photos and audio are processed via Firebase AI Logic with App Check enforcement. Personal data stays on your device — only the analysis result is stored. You can delete everything anytime from Settings.

Does the Cat Translator really work?

Cat meows evolved primarily for human communication — frequency, duration, and pattern carry meaning. Mochi classifies them into known vocalization categories. Treat it as informed entertainment plus useful behavior data.

What's the difference vs other cat apps?

Mochi is cat-only (not dog/cat mixed), all-in-one (scans + litter + translator + chat + vet PDF + journal + memories), and uses Gemini 2.5 multimodal AI for richer, more accurate analysis.

Which devices does Mochi support?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Optimized for iPhone 17 Pro Max but works beautifully on all modern iPhones.
