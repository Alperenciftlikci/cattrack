Frequently asked

Is the AI as accurate as a vet?

No — Mochi is an early-warning tool, not a diagnostic device. The AI flags patterns and concerns to discuss with your vet, who remains the authority on medical decisions.

Does Mochi work with multiple cats?

Yes. Add unlimited cat profiles. The compatibility checker also helps multi-cat households read dynamics.

What happens after the 3-day free trial?

You'll be billed at the rate you selected. Cancel anytime from the App Store before the trial ends to avoid charges.

Is my data private?

Yes. Photos and audio are processed via Firebase AI Logic with App Check enforcement. You can delete all your data anytime from Settings.

Does the Cat Translator really work?

Cat meows evolved primarily for human communication — frequency and pattern carry meaning. Mochi classifies them into known vocalization categories. Treat it as informed entertainment plus useful behavior data.

What's the difference vs TTcare or MeowTalk?

Mochi is cat-only (not dog/cat mixed), all-in-one (scans + litter + translator + chat + vet PDF), and uses Gemini 2.5 multimodal AI for richer analysis.
